Five Suns Digital LLC ("Five Suns Digital," "we," "our," or "us") operates the website fivesunsdigital.com (the "Site") and develops mobile applications, including The Appraisal Room (together with any other apps we publish, the "Apps"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information about you when you visit our Site, use our Apps, or contact us about our services.

By using the Site or our Apps, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the Site and Apps.

Short version: We collect only what we need to provide our services and app features — including using a trusted third-party AI service to generate appraisals when you use The Appraisal Room. We do not sell your personal information. Ever.

03

Our Apps & AI-Powered Features

Some of our mobile applications use artificial intelligence to power core features. This section explains how that works, what we collect to make it possible, and how that data is shared. It applies in addition to the general practices described elsewhere in this policy.

The Appraisal Room

The Appraisal Room lets you photograph an item to receive an estimated valuation. When you use this feature:

  • You choose what to submit. A photo is only captured or uploaded when you actively use the appraisal feature. The app requests camera and/or photo library permission on your device for this purpose; you can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your device's settings.
  • Your photo is sent to a third-party AI provider. To generate an appraisal, the photo and any accompanying details you provide are transmitted over an encrypted connection to Anthropic, a third-party artificial intelligence service, for analysis. Anthropic processes the image to identify the item and produce the descriptive and valuation output the app displays to you.
  • We use the output only to deliver your appraisal. We do not use submitted photos for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to generating the appraisal you requested.
  • Equivalent protection. Our AI service provider is contractually and technically required to protect your data with safeguards equivalent to those described in this policy, and does not use data submitted through our app to train its models outside the terms of our agreement with them.

By taking or uploading a photo using the appraisal feature, you consent to that photo being transmitted to our AI service provider for the purpose described above. If you do not wish to do this, simply don't use that feature of the app.

Looking for the specifics on a different app? As we release new apps or add AI-powered features to existing ones, we will update this section to describe their data practices before — or at the same time as — those features become available.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information, including photos submitted through our Apps, only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers — Trusted third-party vendors who assist us in operating the Site or delivering services (e.g., email hosting, analytics platforms, project management tools). These parties are contractually obligated to use your data only as directed by us and in accordance with this policy.
  • AI Service Providers — When you use AI-powered features in our Apps, such as photo-based appraisals in The Appraisal Room, the relevant photo and request data is shared with Anthropic, our third-party AI processing partner, solely to generate the output you requested. This provider is contractually required to protect your data with safeguards equivalent to those described in this policy.
  • Legal Requirements — If required by law, court order, or government authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Five Suns Digital LLC, our clients, or others.
  • Business Transfers — In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to a successor entity. We will notify you before your data is subject to a different privacy policy.
  • With Your Consent — For any other purpose with your explicit consent.

06

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Specifically:

  • Contact form submissions are retained for up to 3 years from the date of receipt, or until the conclusion of any related business relationship.
  • Project-related communications are retained for the duration of the project plus 5 years for business record-keeping purposes.
  • Analytics data is retained in accordance with the data retention settings of the applicable analytics platform (e.g., Google Analytics default is 26 months).
  • Photos submitted for AI-powered features are retained only as long as necessary to generate and deliver the requested output (such as an appraisal), after which they are deleted from our active systems.

When data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

Colorado Residents

  • Opt out of the sale of personal data
