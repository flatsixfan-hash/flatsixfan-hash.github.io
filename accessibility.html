Five Suns Digital LLC is committed to ensuring that our website, fivesunsdigital.com, is accessible to all visitors — including those who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or voice control software.

We believe that the web should be usable by everyone. Accessibility is not an afterthought for us — it is a design requirement we apply to every project we build, for ourselves and our clients.

If you encounter any barrier on our site, please email us at Sales@fivesunsdigital.com. We will respond within 2 business days and work to address the issue promptly.

01

Our Commitment

Five Suns Digital LLC strives to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. These guidelines, developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), define how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities — including visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive disabilities.

Our accessibility effort is ongoing. As we update and expand fivesunsdigital.com, accessibility is a standing consideration in every change we make.

02

Conformance Status

Partially Conformant — WCAG 2.1 Level AA

Partially conformant means that some content does not yet fully meet the standard. We are actively working to close those gaps. Known limitations are listed in Section 5 of this statement.

Principle Description Status
Perceivable Content is presentable in different ways, including assistive technologies Substantially met
Operable All functionality is available via keyboard and alternative input methods Substantially met
Understandable Content and interface behavior are predictable and error-free Substantially met
Robust Content can be reliably interpreted by assistive technologies Substantially met

03

Technical Specifications

Our website relies on the following technologies to support accessibility:

  • HTML5 — Semantic markup with proper heading hierarchy, landmark regions, and native form controls
  • CSS3 — Responsive layout that adapts to user preferences, including prefers-reduced-motion for vestibular sensitivities
  • WAI-ARIA — ARIA roles, states, and properties where native HTML semantics are insufficient (e.g., interactive portfolio cards)
  • Visible focus indicators — All interactive elements have visible focus styles for keyboard navigation
  • Skip navigation link — A "Skip to Main Content" link is the first focusable element on every page
  • Form labels — All form fields have associated <label> elements linked via for/id attributes
  • Color contrast — Text and UI elements target a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio against background colors

05

Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, the following areas may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. We are actively working to address them:

  • Portfolio images — Some portfolio card images currently lack fully descriptive alternative text. We are in the process of adding comprehensive alt descriptions for all project visuals.
  • Mobile navigation — The hamburger/mobile menu is visually hidden on small screens. Full keyboard and screen reader access for the mobile nav is planned in an upcoming update.
  • Third-party content — Embedded or linked third-party services (such as Formspree for form processing) are outside our direct control. We select third-party partners with accessibility in mind and monitor for issues.

We aim to resolve each of these items within our next scheduled site update. If any of these limitations affect you now, please contact us directly and we will work with you to provide an accessible alternative immediately.

07

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response to an accessibility concern, you have the right to contact the relevant authority in your region:

  • United States — The U.S. Department of Justice Civil Rights Division at ada.gov, or the U.S. Access Board at access-board.gov
  • European Union — Your national supervisory authority under Directive (EU) 2016/2102 on the accessibility of public sector websites
  • United Kingdom — The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) at equalityhumanrights.com

We encourage you to contact us first — we genuinely want to resolve accessibility issues quickly and directly.

08

Assessment Approach

Five Suns Digital LLC assesses the accessibility of this website through the following methods:

  • Self-evaluation — Our development team reviews pages against WCAG 2.1 AA success criteria during development and after significant updates
  • Automated testing — We use browser-based accessibility linters and audit tools (such as the Axe browser extension and Chrome Lighthouse) to flag common issues
  • Manual keyboard testing — We verify that all interactive elements are reachable and operable using keyboard navigation alone
  • Screen reader testing — We periodically test with NVDA on Windows and VoiceOver on macOS/iOS
  • User feedback — Reports from users are treated as high-priority issues and reviewed promptly

This statement was last reviewed on June 3, 2026. We review and update this statement at least annually and whenever significant changes are made to the site.
